Description

Handcrafted by Sudha Paridha using the ancient Dhokra lost-wax technique, this Statement Choker embodies Day One's tribal heritage through its intricate brass work. No machine can replicate this—it is a living expression of handmade craftsmanship at its very best, storytelling. Each piece gives fair-trade revenue and carries agency and transparency, in addition to the beauty of sustainable, handmade expression.
